Our strength and what sets us apart

United Integrated's core strength lies in being the only company in Saudi Arabia to offer this integrated package of services, backed by deep expertise across every aspect of warehouse work — from management and operations through to development and optimisation.

Strengths

Six reasons the difference is tangible

Leadership and distinction

We are the only company in Saudi Arabia to offer this integrated range of advisory and operational logistics services under one roof — so you never need to coordinate several suppliers.

Guaranteed cost reduction

We deliver creative, innovative solutions that cut operating costs by up to 20% — a figure we are prepared to commit to in the contract.
