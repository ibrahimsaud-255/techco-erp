Warehouse Organisation, Layout and Relocation

We are not a furniture removals company — we are warehouse relocation specialists, working to a logistics methodology that protects your stock.

Part one: warehouse organisation and layout (internal improvement)

Organising products well inside the warehouse directly raises operational efficiency: it shortens the time taken to pick or receive goods, lowers the error rate and improves accuracy across every process. When organising and arranging the warehouse, our team takes account of:

  • The nature of the products: designing the storage system — floor, shelving or racking — around the materials being stored.
  • Aisle design and equipment: setting the right aisle width for the handling equipment in use.
  • ABC analysis: storing fast-moving goods in easily accessible locations.
  • Slow-moving and dead stock: allocating upper or rear areas to slow-moving materials.
  • Securing assets: keeping high-value goods in secure, locked locations accessible only to named personnel.

Part two: warehouse relocation and transfer (a complete move)

Moving an entire warehouse from one site to another is a complex, sensitive logistics operation that demands more than simply "moving" — it demands the expertise to protect stock integrity and keep the business running.

We are not a furniture removals company. We are warehouse relocation specialists — and unlike removals firms, we understand the value of inventory and approach the move with a logistics methodology that ensures no goods are damaged or lost in transit.

  • Stock integrity: complete preservation of the quantity and quality of the goods moved.
  • Organisation at the new site: goods arranged professionally and methodically at the new location from day one.
  • Strategic planning: accounting for the nature of the goods, how often they are requested and their volume, so every item is easy to reach the moment operations restart.
