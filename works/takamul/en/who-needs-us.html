Who needs our services?

United Integrated's services suit every type of business — pharmaceutical and medical supplies, spare parts, electronics and food are simply examples.

Who our services are for

The group with most to gain from United Integrated's services

Owners and partners

Shareholders and stakeholders concerned with protecting assets and the accuracy of the financial statements.

Chief executives and general managers

Those seeking lower operating costs and higher productivity without sacrificing service quality.

Finance directors and internal auditors

Those who need a neutral audit reference and a reliable variance report to close the books.

Supply chain and procurement managers

And business development managers seeking a better order cycle and more accurate planning.

Warehouse and showroom managers

Those facing the daily challenges of space, errors, and order fulfilment pressure.

Governance and compliance officers

Those who need effective controls to limit the risk of manipulation and shrinkage.

Facing any of these challenges?

If your answer is "yes" to any of them — we speak your language

Are you seeing recurring variances between physical and book stock?
A balance gap is the first sign of a breakdown in receiving and issuing controls. We start with a neutral diagnostic count, then address the root causes of the variances.
Is your warehouse operating cost climbing year on year?
We identify the sources of waste and re-engineer the procedures — targeting a reduction of up to 20% in operating costs.
Are picking errors and shipping delays holding you back?
We review the full order cycle from receipt to delivery, and apply performance indicators that measure the improvement week by week.
Running out of warehouse space?
Before considering additional space, Layout analysis and ABC classification usually reveal wasted space that can be recovered.
Have a good team but no specialist leadership?
Logistics supervision lets you keep your own team under expert leadership that raises its performance.
Moving to a new warehouse?
We relocate your warehouse with a logistics methodology that protects the stock and has it organised at the new site from day one.
