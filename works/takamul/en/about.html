"United Integrated" is a leading Saudi firm specialising in innovative solutions and consultancy across inventory counting, warehouse management, and all logistics operations.

About

Innovative logistics solutions to improve operations and cut costs

We improve our clients' operations, raise their productivity and cut their operating costs — from a single warehouse to multi-site warehouse networks.

Our creed: a deep understanding of the client's needs and objectives, and delivering the best solutions and advice to ensure those objectives are met.
