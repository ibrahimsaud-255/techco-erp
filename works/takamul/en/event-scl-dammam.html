Supply Chain Leaders Meetup — Dammam

The Supply Chain Leaders Meetup in Dammam, sharing experience on developing logistics operations in the industrial sector.

The United Integrated Logistics team met supply-chain leaders from across the Eastern Province at a specialist gathering in Dammam, attended by representatives of major industrial and commercial organisations.

The meetup addressed warehousing challenges specific to industrial environments: raw material and finished goods stores, controlling material consumption, and tying inventory accuracy to production planning — where any error in a stock balance turns into a production line stoppage or an unnecessary purchase.

We presented our four-stage methodology (assessment, design, implementation, monitoring and follow-up), and made the case that the final stage — on-site follow-up — is what separates a consultancy report that stays in a desk drawer from a solution that is implemented and measured.

  • Challenges in raw material and finished goods warehouses
  • Linking inventory accuracy to production and purchasing plans
  • Why the monitoring and follow-up stage matters after implementation
