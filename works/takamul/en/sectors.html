Sectors we serve and our clients

United Integrated's services suit every type of business and every kind of company, at any scale: a large warehouse, a small store, or a factory warehouse (raw materials and finished goods).

Sectors

Cross-sector experience, one rigorous methodology

Stock differs from one sector to the next, but the principles of accuracy and efficiency are the same — and we adapt our solutions to each sector.

Healthcare and pharmaceuticals

Pharmaceutical and medical supply warehouses, where inventory accuracy and expiry-date tracking demand the highest standards of discipline.

Spare parts and automotive

Spare parts warehouses holding thousands of similar lines, where precise labelling and ABC classification make an immediate difference to picking speed.

Electronics and appliances

High-value goods need secured locations, regular control counts, and strict receiving and issuing controls.

Food and consumer goods

Strict application of FIFO/FEFO, precise stock rotation management, and less waste and damage.

Retail and branch networks

Central warehouses feeding a branch network, where inventory accuracy determines the end customer's experience.

Manufacturing and contracting

Factory warehouses (raw materials and finished goods) and project stores, with controlled materials consumption.

Government and semi-government bodies

Registers and counts of fixed assets and custody items, with neutral reports that stand as a reference for regulators and auditors.

Logistics companies and service providers

Auditing and improving distribution centre operations, and raising the efficiency of operational teams.
