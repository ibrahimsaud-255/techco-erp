Warehouse Operations Improvement and Development

In-depth analysis that turns your warehouse from a cost centre into a core driver of business success, with cost reductions of up to 20%.

From cost centre to growth engine

Every warehouse faces its own challenges, but the objective is the same: lower operating costs and higher operational efficiency. Picking errors, delays at goods-in and poor use of space all eat into your margin. At United Integrated we do not offer off-the-shelf fixes; we deliver a deep, comprehensive analysis that turns your warehouse into a core driver of business success.

Our four-stage methodology

  • Assess: a deep analysis of current operations, identifying weak points and sources of waste.
  • Design: solutions and procedures built specifically for your business to raise efficiency and cut cost.
  • Implement: working alongside you to apply the improvements and train your team on them.
  • Monitor: using key performance indicators (KPIs) to ensure the results are sustained.

The four elements of a successful warehouse operation

  • The system (WMS): how effectively your system supports day-to-day operations.
  • The workforce: the capability, productivity and training of your staff.
  • Policies and procedures: the clarity of the standard operating procedures (SOPs) in use.
  • Layout and planning: how effective the internal layout of the warehouse is.

A full analysis of every warehouse process

1. Improving inbound operations

Correct receiving is the foundation of accurate inventory. We review and improve:

  • How documents (purchase order, invoice) are checked and matched.
  • The speed and accuracy of unloading and sorting goods.
  • Recording variances and mismatches and reporting them immediately.
  • Updating the WMS with received quantities in real time.
  • Moving goods and directing them to the correct storage locations.

2. Improving storage and stock keeping

  • Optimal use of space and reduction of wasted area.
  • Effective application of first in, first out (FIFO).
  • Storing goods safely and correctly, in line with handling instructions.
  • Easy access to goods at the pick face to speed up order preparation.

3. Improving outbound and order preparation

  • Sending the right goods, in the right quantity, to the right customer.
  • Reducing order cycle time from receipt through to despatch.
  • Retaining and properly archiving proofs of delivery (PODs).
  • A clear mechanism for handling and managing customer returns and rejections.

Beyond process: performance and data

Putting key performance indicators (KPIs) in place

You cannot improve what you cannot measure. We help you define and apply the right indicators for your warehouse, such as:

  • Inventory accuracy.
  • Operating cost per square metre.
  • Inventory turnover rate.
  • Average order preparation time.

Staff performance and training

Your people are your most valuable asset. We assess performance on productivity, collaboration and commitment, and deliver on-the-job training that raises team capability and keeps the improvements in place after the project ends.
