Our Methodology

From diagnosis to sustainable results

We do not treat symptoms — we pinpoint the source of the problem and deliver solutions that resolve it at the root, through four sequential phases.

01

Evaluation and Diagnosis

A comprehensive study and analysis of the current state (As-Is) inside the warehouse: analysis of daily practice, benchmarking against best practice, and gap identification in a detailed report.

02

Bespoke Solution Design

Designing solutions that address the root causes, defining the requirements involved (resources, training, technology), and setting out deliverables and indicators — then discussing and fully agreeing them before implementation.

03

Implementation and Change Management

Implementing the solutions on the ground to a clear plan: a defined timeline, key milestones (Milestones), and precise allocation of responsibilities.

04

Monitoring and Follow-up

The most important phase: measuring performance to confirm the results are being delivered, and spotting any deviation and correcting it immediately to sustain them.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what warehouse managers ask most

What sets United Integrated apart from other logistics companies?
We are the only company in Saudi Arabia that delivers this complete package under one roof: consultancy, stock counting, operations and management, supervision, auditing, labelling, and warehouse relocation. Our role does not end with the advisory report — we follow the work on the ground until the agreed objectives are met.
How much could you save on warehouse operating costs?
We deliver innovative solutions that cut operating costs by up to 20% — a figure we are prepared to commit to contractually. Such is our confidence in our solutions that we can tie our fees directly to a share of the savings we generate for you.
Do you offer counting as a neutral third party?
Yes. We act as an independent, neutral third party for counting goods, inventory and fixed assets, and issue a precise variance report between physical and book balances that stands as an audit reference for internal and external auditors.
What types of count do you carry out?
Full annual counts (Wall-to-Wall), periodic cycle counting (Cycle Counting) based on ABC classification, unannounced control counts (Spot Audits), plus fixed-asset registers and counts.
Which sectors do you serve?
All sectors: pharmaceutical and medical supplies, spare parts, electronics, food, retail, contracting, manufacturing and more — and at any scale: a large warehouse, a small store, or a factory warehouse (raw materials and finished goods).
Which cities do you operate in?
We serve clients across every region of Saudi Arabia, with projects delivered in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and the Eastern Province, and Qassim.
